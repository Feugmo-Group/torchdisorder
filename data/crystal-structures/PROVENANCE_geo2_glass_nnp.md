# `geo2_glass_nnp.cif` — provenance

A **published, independently validated** amorphous GeO₂ model. It replaces
`geo2_glass.cif`, which is not a glass (see below).

## Source

S. Kasamatsu et al., *"Comparison of intermediate-range order in GeO₂ glass:
molecular dynamics using machine-learning interatomic potential vs. reverse Monte
Carlo fitting to experimental data"*, **J. Chem. Phys. 161, 204103 (2024)**.
DOI: [10.1063/5.0240087](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0240087)

Data: ISSP Data Repository entry 43 →
`https://isspns-gitlab.issp.u-tokyo.ac.jp/skasamatsu/GeO2_glass_NNP`
File within archive: `GGA_PBE/nvt_ambient/sample_0/CONTCAR`

**License: CC BY 4.0** — reuse permitted with attribution. Cite the paper above.

Converted from VASP `CONTCAR` to CIF with ASE. Generated by the authors via
molecular dynamics with a neural-network potential trained on GGA-PBE DFT.

## Verified properties

Checked here with `torchdisorder.common.validation.validate_structure`, not taken
on trust from the deposit:

| quantity | this file | experiment |
|---|---|---|
| atoms | 3240 (1080 Ge, 2160 O) | — |
| cell | 37.207584 Å cubic | — |
| density | 3.6427 g/cm³ | 3.65 |
| ⟨CN⟩ Ge–O | 4.021 (98.0% four-fold) | 4 |
| min Ge–O | 1.661 Å | no contacts below ~1.5 Å |
| Ge–O bond | 1.7781 ± 0.0486 Å | 1.73–1.75 |
| O–Ge–O | 109.22 ± 8.18° | ~109.5, broad |
| Ge–O–Ge | 128.48 ± 10.18° | 130–135 |

**Two honest caveats.** The Ge–O bond is ~1.78 Å against an experimental
1.73–1.75 — a ~2% overestimate, which is the well-known GGA-PBE tendency to
lengthen bonds, inherited by the NNP trained on it. And Ge–O–Ge sits at 128.5°,
just below the quoted 130–135° range. Neither disqualifies the structure as a
refinement seed, but a refined result should not be read as agreeing with
experiment to better than these offsets.

## The RMC ensemble is also in the archive — and is not the better seed

`RMC/optimized_st_GGA/part_0/CONTCAR` is the same composition and box fitted by
reverse Monte Carlo to the neutron data. Measured here: ⟨CN⟩ = 4.115 with only
**88.5%** four-fold Ge (against 98.0% for the MD ensemble), O–Ge–O spread
±13.25° and Ge–O–Ge ±15.61°. RMC maximises disorder subject to the data, so it
is systematically less tetrahedrally clean. It is a useful comparison target —
it is *the* structure consistent with the measurement — but the MD ensemble is
the better starting point for refinement.

## Matching experimental data

`data/xrd_measurements/GeO2_isotope/{70,73}FQ.dat` are the Ge isotope-substituted
neutron structure factors from the same deposit: Q = 0.40–23.50 Å⁻¹, ΔQ = 0.05,
463 points, two columns after a 2-line header. They are byte-identical across the
five `part_*` directories. Underlying measurement is Salmon's; contact him for
questions about the data itself.

## Why the old file could not be used

`data/crystal-structures/geo2_glass.cif` is **byte-identical** to
`c-GeO2.cif` (md5 `50e867e514603e2a5986eaf60a724bcc`) — it is the crystal under a
glass filename. ρ = 4.043 against the experimental 3.65, and an O–Ge–O spread of
±2.86°, which is crystalline rather than glassy. Every GeO₂ result in this repo
was therefore seeded from the crystal scaled to glass density.
